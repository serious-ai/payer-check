See what each insurance company really costs your practice

Denials, visits paid at a lower level than you billed, payments short of your contract, and the staff time spent chasing all of it, added up payer by payer. Free to use, and it runs entirely on your own computer.

Your files never leave your computer

  • Nothing is uploaded, not to us and not to anyone else.
  • No sign-up, no account, no email address.
  • It works with your internet turned off.

This page works like a calculator that lives inside your web browser. Your billing files are read right here on your screen, and the results save back to your own computer. Want to see for yourself? Once this page has loaded, turn off your Wi-Fi and run the check. It works the same.

For your IT team: how to verify this in a few minutes
  1. Save this page, disconnect from the network, open the saved copy and run it. It works the same.
  2. Open the browser's developer tools, go to the Network panel and run a check. The list stays empty.
  3. Read the page's first lines. Its Content Security Policy (default-src 'none'; connect-src 'none') tells the browser to block every fetch, upload and outside file the page could ask for. The policy doesn't govern the page sending the tab elsewhere, so the page has no links and no code that opens another site.
  4. Read the source. It's one file with nothing loaded from anywhere, and the code is public with an automated test that fails the build on any network request.

Each anesthesia line is priced as (base units + time units + any physical status units) times the payer's conversion factor, times the share its modifier pays. A gap is split into the reason that explains it, in the order of the columns from right to left.

The two summaries hold totals only, with no patient-level fields. The chart-pull list (and the anesthesia pull list) has a row for every flagged line with its patient account number, so keep it on this computer.

What it looks at

Only what's on the payment files

From each payment file: the insurance company, each claim's account number, status and date received, the treating provider, and each line's codes, modifiers, units, dates, amounts and adjustment codes. It skips the patient's name. No AI runs here, only plain calculations anyone can read in this page's code.
